NEXA VIEW - User Registration Agreement

Last Updated: August 14, 2026

1. About NEXA VIEW

NEXA VIEW is an online platform where users earn rewards by completing daily tasks such as watching videos and advertisements.

2. Eligibility

You must be 18+ years old and have a valid Kenyan phone number to register. 1 Account per phone number only.

3. Earnings & Tasks

1. Earnings are based on completing available daily tasks. Earnings are not guaranteed.
2. Task rewards depend on active advertisers. We may pause tasks at any time.
3. Sunday is a rest day. No tasks available.

4. Packages

Buying a package unlocks daily tasks. Package fees are non-refundable. Package duration and daily earnings are shown before purchase.

5. Withdrawals

1. Minimum Withdrawal: Ksh 200
2. Time: 9:00AM - 5:00PM Daily. 1 Withdrawal per day.
3. Tax: 15% processing fee will be deducted from all withdrawals.
4. Payouts are sent to the M-PESA number in your account within 24 hours.
5. We reserve the right to delay withdrawals for security checks.

6. Prohibited Actions

Creating multiple accounts, using bots, or fake activity will lead to permanent ban and forfeiture of balance.

7. Termination

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates these terms.

8. Contact

For support: WhatsApp us via the "INFO" tab in the app.
